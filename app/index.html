First, Frame the Deep Research Prompt

(Context Matters)

Find out more about your organization challenge than ever before, without the need of consultants, market reports or SMEs. You no longer need to be an expert researcher; all you need is context mining.

Prompt:

I am part of the [Department/Business Unit] at [Organization Name], and I want to conduct deep research into organization challenge I am facing. Help me understand the broader context of this challenge, including its key drivers, how similar challenges are being addressed, and where AI could help solve or improve the problem. I want the research to be tailored to my specific role, context, and needs, rather than providing generic insights. Here is some context about my situation: [Participants: Add relevant context, details, constraints, existing processes, tools, stakeholders, or other information specific to your domain.]
